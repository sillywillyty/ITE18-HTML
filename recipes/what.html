Cheese Pizza Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 (14 ounce) package refrigerated pizza crust
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).
  2. Roll out the pizza crust onto a lightly floured surface. Transfer to a lightly greased pizza pan or baking sheet. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the crust, leaving a small border around the edges. Sprinkle shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly. Remove from oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing.
